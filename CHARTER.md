# Charter: what a people believes, and who it lets in

**This exists so that adding a machine to the mesh adds a people, and the network
between the machines is the border between their cultures.**

Opened 2026-08-04. Revised the same day, after the design discussion recorded in
`REVISIONS` at the end of this file. It follows a closed artificial-life track,
whose reasons are in `hecate-biotope/WHY_THIS_TRACK_CLOSED.md`. It keeps that
track's substrate, its instruments and its discipline. It keeps none of its
biology.

---

## The one idea

Cultural evolution has fifty years of formal models and every one of them runs on
a graph that somebody drew. Axelrod's agents sit on a lattice the modeller chose.
Bounded-confidence models connect whoever the modeller connected. Cultural group
selection needs group boundaries that survive contact, and in every published
model those boundaries are an assumption.

Here they are not drawn. **An island is a machine, the reach between islands is
the mesh, and a partition is a barrier that nobody chose.** Each island decides
for itself who may land, and the policy it decides with is its own.

That is the contribution. Not that agents hold beliefs, which exists everywhere.

## Two schools, and this track takes both

There are two answers in the literature to what drives culture, and they are
usually treated as rivals.

**Cultural materialism** (Harris) says infrastructure determines structure
determines superstructure: what a society believes is explained by how it
produces and reproduces. **Dual inheritance theory** (Boyd, Richerson, Henrich)
says culture is a second inheritance system with its own dynamics, spreading by
conformity, prestige and content bias with some independence from material
conditions.

**They are not rivals here. They are two levels of one stack.**

> **Material conditions are the selective environment. Transmission dynamics are
> the search process.**

Harris describes the landscape. Boyd and Richerson describe how variants move on
it. That is the same structure genetic evolution has, an environment plus a
variation-and-selection machinery, which is a reason to believe the split is real
rather than a compromise.

**And the same fork appears one level down, in economics, which is corroboration
rather than coincidence.** Economic anthropology spent thirty years on
**formalism against substantivism**: formalists held that maximisation under
scarcity is universal, substantivists (Polanyi) that economies are embedded in
kinship, religion and politics and take culturally specific forms. When one seam
shows up independently in two fields, it is usually a boundary between levels and
not a disagreement about one.

So this model is **formalist at the person and substantivist at the island**.
Each person has a decision process. What that process pursues, and through which
institutions, is cultural.

⚠ **The fork is named rather than settled, and it must not be settled by
accident.** Build order will answer it silently if nobody watches: a track that
builds the material axes first and finds that beliefs follow has tested Harris,
whichever way it reports. Every pre-registration states which of the two its
claim bears on.

## What culture is, and how you could tell it is here

Culture is a **second inheritance system**. It moves sideways between living
persons rather than down from parents, it moves within one life rather than at
birth, and what it carries can outlive everyone who was carrying it.

| | genetic, vertical, fixed at birth | cultural, horizontal, within a life |
|---|---|---|
| what travels | the network that turns what is sensed into what is done | the **model** and the **doctrine** |
| written by | mutation and recombination | experience, and other persons |
| dies with its holder | yes | not if it was taught to somebody |

**The first instrument comes from that definition and not from a hypothesis.** A
belief still held after every person that held it at time `T` is dead has been
inherited by something other than birth. If nothing here ever does that, there is
no second system, there is genetic evolution with the word culture written on it,
and no later result means anything.

The predecessor spent twenty-four worlds asking why signalling never appeared,
having built no act that could write to the world. **So before any claim about
what is believed, this repo publishes the count of things said.** A run in which
nothing was ever transmitted is invalid, not null.

## Beliefs have two levels, and their disagreement is the subject

Ruled 2026-08-04.

| | what it is | who may write to it |
|---|---|---|
| **model** | what this person's own experience taught them about how the world behaves | observation, and nothing else |
| **doctrine** | what this person was told, held with a confidence | other persons, and nothing else |

Behaviour comes from both. **The gap between them is the phenomenon**: dissonance,
conversion, radicalisation, and the conditions under which evidence defeats what
you were taught or fails to. A belief held in spite of the evidence is where
everything interesting lives, and a single merged belief store cannot represent
one.

**Doctrine is a set, not a slot.** It maps propositions to confidences, and being
told something writes an entry without clearing the others. A person can
therefore hold two doctrines in tension as well as a doctrine against their own
model, which is what conversion actually looks like from the inside and what
syncretism looks like from outside. This gives a third comparison for free:
model against doctrine, and doctrine against doctrine.

A neural network was considered and rejected **for holding beliefs**, and that
rejection stands: a network maps a situation onto a response and cannot hold a
proposition that can be stated, contested, taught, or believed against what is in
front of it. Where the network does belong is below, under *the person is a
network*.

**A proposition must be checkable against the world.** It has the shape *acting
this way in that situation yields this outcome*, and the world either behaves that
way or does not. This is a design requirement before it is anything else: if a
proposition has no truth in the world then the model cannot contradict the
doctrine, and the only thing worth measuring here is unmeasurable.

## What a person needs: nine axes, none of them first

Ruled 2026-08-04. The axes are **Max-Neef's fundamental human needs**, taken
whole:

**subsistence, protection, affection, understanding, participation, leisure,
creation, identity, freedom.**

Three of his claims are adopted with them, and each does real work here.

**Needs are universal and finite; satisfiers are cultural.** Max-Neef's central
claim is that the nine are the same in every culture and every historical period,
and that what varies is the *means* by which they are met. That is *give the plan,
evolve the parameters*, already formalised by an economist in 1986. It also
replaces a weaker idea this charter briefly held. **A culture is not a weighting
over the axes. A culture is a set of satisfiers**, which is to say what this
people actually does about each need. A weighting is a number. A satisfier is a
practice, and a practice can be taught, argued over, imposed and abandoned.

**They are simultaneous, not hierarchical.** Unlike Maslow, no need is
foundational and none is a luxury. Subsistence is not built first because it is
not first.

**They are incommensurable.** Sen's capability approach holds that no permanent
priority or relative weight can be assigned between plural human achievements,
and he won a Nobel arguing it against utility maximisation.

⚠ **Incommensurability is a commitment, not an inconvenience.** The moment nine
needs are collapsed into one number so that something has a quantity to maximise,
this model has adopted the position Sen spent a career refuting and has destroyed
the thing that makes cultures different from each other. **There is no scalar
happiness in this model and there will not be one.**

### The rule that keeps nine axes cheap

> **No need may have code of its own.**

Nine needs are expensive only if each gets bespoke machinery. They are nearly free
if **a person's needs are a vector, a satisfier is an effect profile over that
vector, and a culture is a set of prescribed satisfiers**. Adding a tenth need is
then data, not a subsystem.

The moment subsistence gets a special case that affection does not have, Maslow's
hierarchy has been smuggled back in through the implementation, and Max-Neef's
whole point is lost to a convenience.

### A satisfier can lie, and that is measurable

Max-Neef classifies satisfiers as **synergic** (meet one need and others with it),
**singular**, **inhibiting** (over-meet one and impair others), **pseudo-satisfiers**
(give a false sense of satisfaction), and **violators** (claim to meet a need
while making it harder to meet, including others).

**This is the mechanism by which a culture can be measurably bad for the people
carrying it, without anybody declaring from outside which cultures are bad.** A
doctrine that prescribes a satisfier claiming to serve identity while destroying
affection is a violator, and violator is a *measurement*: it compares what the
satisfier promises against what the need vector actually does. Cultural pathology
becomes an observation rather than a judgement.

## The economy, and what is actually scarce

Needs plus scarce means plus a choice about allocation is the definition of an
economy. This one has three quantities and **they must not be joined**.

**What is scarce is attention.** A person attends to only so many others per tick
and lives only so long, so an island generates a fixed number of learning events
set by population, lifespan and learning rate. Every slot one variant fills is one
another did not. That is the structure energy had in the predecessor: finite,
arriving at a rate, contested, and the thing everything is priced in.

It also gives `Ne` a physical meaning rather than a statistical one. **Cultural
`Ne` is how many distinct teachers fill a person's slots**, which makes the
quantity that killed the last track a consequence of numbers this one sets.

**What is fitness is the count of learners.** An agent's cultural fitness for a
variant is settled in the literature: it is the number of learners to whom it
transmits that variant. Not its wellbeing, not its survival, not its offspring.
Directly countable, and this charter already named it as an instrument without
noticing it was the fitness function.

**What is welfare is the need vector, and it is a separate reading.** The field's
central finding is that cultural variants can be optimised for their own spread
rather than for the welfare of their carriers. **If welfare were the fitness
function, that would be unrepresentable by construction, and this would be a model
in which culture is necessarily good for the persons in it.** Keeping the two
apart is what
makes a parasitic culture expressible, and it is the same shape as the
model-doctrine split: two things usually assumed to agree, held apart so their
disagreement can be measured.

### The material base, at the smallest honest size

Subsistence and protection need material conditions, and material conditions were
the energy economy that consumed twenty-four worlds. The minimum that is still
honest:

**Carrying capacity is one number, not a resource field.** An island supports so
many persons; being over it means subsistence goes unmet for somebody. No grid, no
food chemistry, no metabolism, no first law. And `max_persons` **already exists in
`border.erl`** as the reason an island turns somebody away, which makes the
border's *we are full* a material fact rather than an arbitrary policy. That is
cultural materialism in miniature, at the cost of a number already written.

### Three economic forms, as a target rather than a setting

Polanyi identified three modes of economic integration: **reciprocity,
redistribution, and market exchange.** Which mode an island arrives at is a
finding. Nothing here imposes one, and *an island evolved redistribution* is the
kind of result this design exists to be able to produce.

## The person is a network, and the network is not the beliefs

The rejection of neural networks above was about **beliefs**. Behaviour is where
a network belongs, and it is unbounded in size and topology by intent.

```
SENSORS                            HIDDEN              ACTUATORS
  my nine need levels              evolved,            act    choose a satisfier
  my model    (what I saw)         unbounded in        speak  emit a proposition
  my doctrine (what I was told)    size and shape      listen take one in
  what those near me profess                           move   attempt a crossing
  how well those near me fare                          (later) violence
  the island: capacity, crowding
```

The belief stores are **not weights**. They are data the network reads and writes:
doctrine is written by other persons' `speak`, the model is written by the world
answering a prediction, the need levels are written by the world answering an
`act`.

Which gives the sentence this whole architecture rests on:

> **Weights pass at birth. Beliefs pass by speech.**

Dual inheritance implemented as two structurally distinct channels in code rather
than as a metaphor. It is the first thing in this design the predecessor could not
have expressed at all.

### The transmission biases evolve; they are not handed over

An earlier draft gave conformity, prestige and content bias as capacities with
evolvable weight. Better: **give the sensor and the actuator and let the bias
evolve.** The network can see what those near it profess and how well they fare,
and whether it comes to copy the majority, or the successful, or neither, is then
a result rather than a setting.

This makes CLAIM 1 considerably stronger. As written, *conformist transmission
holds between-group variation above neutral* is half stipulated when conformity is
hand-coded. If conformity has to appear first, the claim becomes: does it evolve,
and does it then hold variation.

⚠ **And the risk is real.** The predecessor is a catalogue of things that could
have evolved and did not. If conformity never appears, CLAIM 1 yields a null that
means nothing. **So CLAIM 1 runs both arms, given and evolved, and reports both.**
That is not hedging: the given arm is the control that tells you whether the
evolved arm's null is about conformity or about the population it was run in.

## Life, death, and violence

**A life is presence, and it has a natural end.** A person is in the population or
is not. Everyone dies of age, regardless of violence. Death removes them and
everything they had not taught, so the only route to persistence is having taught
somebody.

⚠ **Natural mortality is load-bearing and is not flavour.** If the only death were
violent, a peaceful island could never show that a belief outlived its holder, the
first instrument would be unsatisfiable without killing, and *did a culture appear*
and *did violence appear* would be the same measurement. And death is what makes
teaching matter at all: without it everyone keeps their own beliefs forever and
there is no second system to observe.

**Violence is between islands first, within an island second.** Raiding uses the
border and migration machinery that already exists. Within-island punishment,
which is what makes conformist transmission stable in the literature, needs
something that does not exist yet: who can reach whom inside an island. They are
different mechanisms with different predictions and are built apart, because world
23 bundled three changes and could not attribute its own result.

**Conquest needs no mechanism of its own.** Once the transmission biases exist, a
victorious population that is numerous or that visibly fares well is copied by the
ordinary machinery. **Imposition is what conformity and prestige look like from
inside a population that has just lost.** Nothing is hand-written, and it buys the
heretic for free: whether a given person converts depends on their own evolved
biases, so someone resists without anybody building resistance.

It also collapses a distinction that looked fundamental. **A raid is migration
that does not ask.** The border can refuse a migrant; it cannot refuse somebody who
comes anyway, or refusing costs it something. Border and violence are one seam
looked at twice.

**Still open, and deliberately:** what an encounter does mechanically, meaning who
dies and who ends up standing where. That is not a payoff to be designed. It is
circumstance, and the outcome should fall out of numbers, beliefs and who showed
up, not out of a table somebody wrote.

## What is given, and what must emerge

**Given**, said out loud to be scaffolding: the two-level belief store and who may
write to each; the proposition space and its truth in the world; the nine need
axes; the act of speaking, listening and acting; islands, migration, the border
seam, island identity; carrying capacity; the mesh edge; the network's sensor and
actuator lists.

**Emerges:** what is believed and how strongly. **Which satisfiers a people
arrives at for each need.** Whether the transmission biases appear at all, and
which. Whom to listen to. The network's size and shape. Which of Polanyi's
economic modes an island lands on. What an island admits, because **admission
policy is expected to become political rather than technical**, and the reason
vocabulary is open on purpose.

## The instruments, named before the mechanisms

Deciding these first is the whole reason the last track's negatives were worth
anything, and *did a culture emerge* is a far softer question than *did a brain
evolve*.

| instrument | answers |
|---|---|
| **utterances per window** | was anything ever said. Zero invalidates the run |
| **transmission chain depth** | did a belief outlive the person it started in |
| **cultural fitness** | how many learners a variant reached. The fitness function, counted |
| **`F_ST` on beliefs, between islands** | is there between-group variation at all |
| **the neutral expectation of `F_ST`** | how much of it random copying explains, at the *measured* migration rate |
| **model-doctrine divergence** | how far what is taught has drifted from what is seen |
| **doctrine-doctrine divergence** | how many incompatible things a person has been told |
| **behavioural attribution** | how much of what a person does comes from doctrine rather than from their own model |
| **the need vector, unaggregated** | how each of the nine is faring. **Never summed** |
| **satisfier classification** | how many satisfiers in use are synergic, and how many are violators |
| **cultural `Ne`** | how many distinct teachers fill a person's attention |

## `Ne` is the binding constraint on everything ambitious here

⚠ **Read this before proposing anything large.**

`Ne` was guessed for twenty-two worlds and measured once, at **7.44** against a
census of 87.95, which put the drift floor at **6.72%** and made nearly every
mechanism ever priced there invisible.

**And the predecessor already ran the experiment that ambition suggests.** `J.1`
said brains stay small because the world asks only one question. World 23 gave
them a second requirement, and it was **refuted**: carriers ran 0 to 2% and the
number of ways of living explored *fell* from 81 to 64. `B.10`, that memory pays,
was refuted worse: memory was not unused, it made computation **less affordable to
have**.

**Richness was not the binding constraint. `Ne` was.** Nine axes will not grow a
network on their own, and an unbounded topology is reachable only when a marginal
unit of computation buys more than the drift floor.

**What this track has that the last one structurally could not:** the cultural
brain hypothesis holds that brains expand in response to the availability of
information and the cost of getting it, and that social transmission is far
cheaper than working things out alone. The predecessor had no social learning at
all, so a network could only ever be built by mutation and every neuron had to pay
for itself against drift. **With teaching, the cost of acquiring a good policy
stops being the cost of evolving one.** That is a different economics of
computation, and it is the honest reason to think a large network is reachable
here.

It also vindicates the subsistence axis on evidence rather than taste: that
hypothesis says brains expand in response to information **and calories**. The
predecessor had calories and no information. This design has information and, in
carrying capacity, a thin honest version of calories.

## The rules this track runs under

1. **A mechanism is chosen by its differential against the measured floor, before
   it is built.** Under the floor means do not build it, or raise `Ne` first.
2. **The floor is a live published instrument.** A run below the level at which
   the effect under study could have been seen **declares itself invalid** rather
   than producing a null that then gets interpreted.
3. **A capacity that was never exercised is not evidence of anything.** Publish
   the exercise count beside every null.
4. **Give the plan, evolve the parameters.** The pre-registration says plainly
   which is which.
5. **One claim at a time.** World 23 bundled three changes and could not attribute
   its own result.
6. **A guard compares two sides of a boundary.** A field computed and never put on
   a wire happened five times in one day and no test of either side could find it.
7. **Smaller in code than what it replaces.** If it is not, the old habits came
   along.
8. **No need may have code of its own.** Nine axes are data. The first bespoke
   case is Maslow's hierarchy arriving through the back door.
9. **Nothing is aggregated into a single number that stands for a person's
   welfare.** Incommensurability is the commitment, and a scalar would quietly
   discard it.
10. **Every register entry carries an ELI5 section.** See below.

### Rule 10, and why it is a rule rather than a courtesy

Every entry in `REGISTER.md` carries a plain-language section explaining the
finding to somebody who knows none of this.

**Written in the same commit as the entry, by whoever wrote the entry.** The
sibling practice on `beam-campus-net` keeps the plain-language layer in a separate
repository, written later, and its own standing warning is that a published post
still asserting a later-refuted finding is the worst failure that project can
have. **An explanation written elsewhere and afterwards is a translation, and
translations drift.** One written in the same commit cannot.

And it is a comprehension test rather than an act of outreach. **If the finding
cannot be explained without the vocabulary, it is not yet understood**, and the
register's most expensive entries are the ones where somebody believed they
understood something they had only named.

## The ethical boundary, on day one

This model contains no human beings. Its persons have no interests, nothing at
stake, and no history. **Nothing observed here is evidence about any human
population, any migration policy, or any real culture**, and the fact that it will
produce suggestive pictures is exactly why this paragraph is in the founding
document rather than added later.

- **Incommensurability does most of the work, structurally.** Because a culture is
  a set of satisfiers and the nine axes are never summed, **there is no number in
  this model that says one island is happier than another.** That is not a
  precaution bolted on to protect us, it is the honest structure, and it removes
  the most dangerous artifact this project could produce.
- **One member of a population is a `person`, and the plural is `persons`.**
  Decided 2026-08-04, over `mind`, `soul` and `agent`, and argued twice on the
  day this opened. `mind` was chosen first, on exactly the grounds this section
  is about: "persons refused" is easier to screenshot into a claim about human
  borders than "minds refused" is.
  It was changed once it was settled that **persons will eventually kill each
  other here**, since between-group lethal conflict is a load-bearing mechanism
  in cultural group selection and not an ugly extra. A model that kills things
  should use the noun where killing sounds like killing. *Twelve minds were
  destroyed* is the sentence you write when you would rather not say what
  happened, and this document's whole discipline is stating the negative in
  advance. **So the risk is accepted rather than dodged, and answered here and in
  how every claim is worded, not by a softer noun.** If a picture of what this
  model does is ever embarrassing, that is information worth having, and a
  euphemism would hide the one signal worth keeping.
- **Vocabulary, because it will drift otherwise.** An island holds **a people**,
  always with the article and always meaning the group. A member is a **person**
  and several are **persons**, never "people". A human being who runs a node is
  an **operator** or an **owner**, never a person. The three words are distinct on
  purpose and formal English already keeps them apart.
- Every claim is stated as *in this model, under these rules, X*. Never as *X*.
- **Propositions are about the physics of the model world.** No religion, no
  politics, no ethnicity, no nationality enters the proposition space. Labelling
  the beliefs with real contested human positions would turn a study of
  transmission into a simulator of persuasion about those positions, and the
  design requirement above already forbids it for an independent reason: a real
  political proposition has no truth value in this world, so nothing could be
  measured.
- **The nine need axes are not an exception to that.** They name what a person
  pursues, never what a person believes. A satisfier is a practice in this model's
  own world and carries no real-world content.
- The border is in the model because group boundaries are the mechanism cultural
  group selection formally requires. **That is a statement about a model and not a
  position about borders.**
- No result from here is published in a form that invites the reading *our
  simulation shows open borders cause X*. A toy that gets quoted that way does
  real damage.

## What carries across, and what does not

**Carried in code**, copied rather than extracted, because two consumers is a copy
and the biotope track is closed so divergence costs nothing. Already ported:
`society_identity`, which separates an island's name from its 128 bits;
`society_mesh`, the only module that knows macula exists; `border.erl` and its
open reason vocabulary. Still owed: the crossing codec with its conservation
ledger and at-most-once crossing id, and the archipelago viewer in
`beam-campus-net`.

**Carried as discipline:** pre-registration with the instrument named and the
negative stated in advance, the register including its `I` entries about how the
work itself went wrong, the archive, and boundary guards.

**Dropped:** the energy economy. Food, metabolism, structure, thirst, predation
and water are biology, and what replaces subsistence here is one number of
carrying capacity rather than a resource field.

⚠ **The refinement this revision makes, because the earlier wording was half
right.** It said attention and time are the cultural analogues of an energy budget
and are *not the same thing*. Both halves stand, and the boundary between them is
now sharper. **The accounting carries: attention is finite, arrives at a rate, and
is contested, and everything is priced in it.** What does not carry is metabolism.
Attention is not consumed to stay alive, there is no first law over it, and
nothing starves. Treating it as food is still the category error. Treating it as
scarce is not.

**The register keeps its numbering.** `G.10` and `I.21` and the rest are
cross-referenced from everywhere, and the one asset worth carrying whole is the
one a renumbering would break. The break is marked, not hidden.

## Owed before the first claim

- **A ruling on how a proposition is identified across islands**, since two
  islands that invent the same idea independently either share an identifier or do
  not, and `F_ST` means something different in each case.
- **Where new persons come from**, and whether a child inherits its parent's
  doctrine or starts blank. That is vertical against horizontal transmission and
  it is a real fork in the literature, not a detail.
- **What an encounter does mechanically.** Who dies, who ends up standing where.
  Circumstance, not a payoff table.
- **Trust.** A hostile node can inject persons and can inject doctrine. Island
  identity defeats accident and not impersonation: nothing signs it today. Naming
  the problem is in scope here, solving it is not.
- **A ruling on the record.** A world spanning nodes is not a pure function of a
  seed and cannot be replayed. The event store replaces replay.

**Considered and not adopted as the axes.** Self-determination theory's three
needs, autonomy, competence and relatedness, map remarkably onto machinery this
design already requires, and its internalisation continuum, external through
introjected and identified to integrated, is a worked-out account of how an
imposed rule becomes part of who you are, which is exactly this model's
doctrine-to-model pipeline. It was not adopted as the axes because it decomposes
**how a pursuit relates to the self** rather than **what is pursued**, and the
second is what a culture prescribes. It remains the strongest candidate for
describing the *quality* of a satisfier once the axes exist, and its universality
is contested in a way worth knowing: the standing critique is that autonomy
encodes Western individualism, and the standing defence is that autonomy means
acting from your own values, which can include honouring an obligation.
