# Life, death and violence

**Decision: a life is presence with a natural end. Violence is between islands
first and within an island second. Conquest needs no mechanism of its own.**

Decided 2026-08-04, in that order, one question at a time.

---

## A life is presence, and it has a natural end

A person is in the population or is not. **Everyone dies of age, regardless of
violence.** Death removes them and everything they had not taught, so the only
route to persistence is having taught somebody.

### Why natural mortality is load-bearing and not flavour

Two reasons, and the first is a measurement argument rather than a realism one.

**If the only death were violent, *did a culture appear* and *did violence appear*
would be the same measurement.** The first instrument in `CHARTER.md` is whether a
belief outlives every person who held it. That requires people to die. In a world
where the only death is killing, a peaceful island can never demonstrate culture at
all, and every positive result would be confounded with the thing we most want to
study separately.

**And death is what makes teaching matter.** Without it everyone keeps their own
beliefs forever, nothing needs to be passed on, and there is no second inheritance
system to observe. Mortality is not a cost imposed on the model. It is the reason
culture is necessary in it.

### What this makes of lifespan

⚠ **Lifespan is the `Ne` dial, and that is new.** Cultural `Ne` is how many models
a person samples from in a life, so lifespan sets it directly, `Ne` sets the drift
floor, and the drift floor decides whether anything is measurable.

The predecessor could not do this. `H.13` was refuted there because lifespan turned
out **not to be controllable**: driving metabolism from 20 down to 1 made life
shorter rather than longer. Here there is no metabolism in the way and lifespan is
simply a number. **The quantity that killed the last track becomes a knob**, and it
ships with the image, never in a node config, per the rule that cost beam01 two
hours.

## Violence: between islands first, within an island second

**Raiding** uses the border and migration machinery that already exists, so it
needs no new notion of who can reach whom. It is also the engine of cultural group
selection in the literature: lethal intergroup conflict is what makes between-group
differences matter rather than merely exist.

**Within-island punishment** is the mechanism that makes conformist transmission
stable, so it is load-bearing rather than decorative. But it needs something that
does not exist: an island today is an undifferentiated population with no space and
no social graph, so *who can kill you* has no answer.

They are different mechanisms with different predictions and are built apart,
because world 23 bundled three changes and could not attribute its own result.

⚠ **In a raid, persons still die.** What sits at the group level is the decision
and the payoff, not the dying. Group-level placement is not a bloodless
abstraction, and it was nearly mis-sold as one in the discussion that produced this
document.

## Conquest needs no mechanism of its own

This is the strongest result of that discussion and it came from the observation
that a raid's outcome should be circumstance rather than a designed payoff.

Once the transmission biases exist, a victorious population that is **numerous** or
that **visibly fares well** is copied by the ordinary machinery: conformist bias
copies the majority, prestige bias copies the successful.

> **Imposition is what conformity and prestige look like from inside a population
> that has just lost.**

Nothing is hand-written. And it buys **the heretic for free**: whether a given
person converts depends on their own evolved biases, so someone resists without
anybody building resistance. A rule that said *conquest overwrites belief* could
never have produced that.

### A raid is migration that does not ask

The border can refuse a migrant. It cannot refuse somebody who comes anyway, or
refusing costs it something. **Border and violence are one seam looked at twice**,
and `border.erl` already exists.

It also gives a genuine prediction. If conquest and immigration run through the
same machinery, they differ only in ratio and direction. One migrant arriving among
a hundred conforms to them; a hundred arriving among ten are conformed to. **So the
conformist bias that preserves between-group variation under trickle migration
destroys it under mass movement**, and somewhere between there is a threshold.
Locating it on a topology nobody drew is what this project is for.

## Rejected on the record

**Capture where the captured keep everything.** The intuitive answer: you win, you
take persons, they keep their model and their doctrine. But you have then imported a
population that believes what your rival believed, so the raid **lowers**
between-group variation. It works directly against the first claim, and a mechanism
whose main effect is to erase the quantity being measured cannot be built first.

**Pure attrition, where a raid only kills.** Every raid shrinks the archipelago, the
archipelago is already small, and small is exactly what killed the last track.
Killing lowers the census, which lowers `Ne`, which raises the floor, which makes
every mechanism including raiding itself harder to see.

## Still open, and deliberately

**What an encounter does mechanically:** who dies, and who ends up standing where.

That is not a payoff to be designed. It is circumstance, and the outcome should
fall out of numbers, beliefs and who showed up, rather than out of a table somebody
wrote. Writing one would be the same error as imposing geography, which world 23
did, and which culled.

⚠ **And whether raiding pays must be measured, never tuned.** If constants are
picked so that raiding comes out balanced, the result has been stipulated. Set the
mechanics on a defensible basis, measure whether raiding invades, and publish it
either way. **If it collapses into universal raiding, that is a real finding**: it
would be the predecessor's boom-bust to mutual extinction replicating on a
substrate with no metabolism at all, which is worth more than a tidy equilibrium.
