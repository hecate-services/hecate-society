# Two schools, at two scales

**Decision: take both. Material conditions are the selective environment,
transmission dynamics are the search process.**

Decided 2026-08-04. `CHARTER.md` states it; this says why, and names the fork it
leaves open.

---

## The two answers

There are two answers in the literature to what drives culture, and they are
usually treated as rivals.

**Cultural materialism** (Marvin Harris) says infrastructure determines structure
determines superstructure: what a society believes is explained by how it produces
and reproduces.

**Dual inheritance theory** (Boyd, Richerson, Henrich) says culture is a second
inheritance system with its own dynamics, spreading by conformity, prestige and
content bias with some independence from material conditions.

## They are not rivals here. They are two levels of one stack

Harris describes the landscape. Boyd and Richerson describe how variants move on
it. That is the same structure genetic evolution has, an environment plus a
variation-and-selection machinery, which is a reason to believe the split is real
rather than a compromise between two positions we did not want to choose between.

## The corroboration, and it is what convinced me

**The same fork appears one level down, in economics.** Economic anthropology
spent thirty years on **formalism against substantivism**. Formalists held that
maximisation under scarcity is universal and applies to any society. Substantivists,
following Polanyi, held that economies are *embedded* in kinship, religion and
politics, and take culturally specific forms: reciprocity, redistribution, market
exchange.

When one seam shows up independently in two fields, it is usually a boundary
between levels and not a disagreement about one.

So this model is **formalist at the person and substantivist at the island**. Each
person has a decision process. What that process pursues, and through which
institutions, is cultural.

## The fork this leaves open, named on purpose

⚠ **Which of the two actually drives culture here is not settled by taking both,
and it must not be settled by accident.**

Build order will answer it silently if nobody watches. A track that builds the
material axes first and then finds that beliefs follow has tested Harris, whichever
way it reports the result, and a track that builds transmission first and finds
that beliefs move regardless of conditions has tested Boyd and Richerson.

**So every pre-registration states which of the two its claim bears on.** That
requirement is in `claims/README.md` and it exists for this paragraph alone.
