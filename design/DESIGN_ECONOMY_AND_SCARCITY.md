# The economy, and what is actually scarce

**Decision: attention is the scarce contested quantity, cultural fitness is the
count of learners, and welfare is a third thing held apart from both.**

Decided 2026-08-04. Needs plus scarce means plus a choice about allocation is the
definition of an economy, so this design has one whether or not it says so. This
says so.

---

## Why this document exists

Three questions in a row could not be answered: what does killing cost, what does
it gain, what makes one island beat another. Each time the answer had to be
invented rather than derived.

**That is what happens when a currency is missing.** Energy in the predecessor was
not a feature, it was the thing that made every other price sayable: a mouth cost
0.24%, an act 1.34%, silencing 10.6%, all against a measured drift floor.

## Energy was doing two jobs, and in culture they come apart

Energy was **the currency**, what acts cost and what makes generalism expensive,
and it was **the fitness**, since differential reproduction on energy is what
selection acted through. Those are one thing in biology because a creature that
runs out of energy stops reproducing.

**In cultural evolution they separate, and that separation is most of the field.**
Looking for a single analogue of energy would produce a model that quietly
collapses them again.

## What is scarce: attention

A person attends to only so many others per tick and lives only so long, so an
island generates a fixed number of learning events set by **population, lifespan
and learning rate**. Every slot one variant fills is one another did not.

That is structurally what energy was: finite, arriving at a rate, contested, and
the thing everything else is priced in.

**It also gives `Ne` a physical meaning rather than a statistical one.** Cultural
`Ne` is how many distinct teachers fill a person's slots, which makes the quantity
that killed the predecessor a consequence of three numbers this track sets.

⚠ **The boundary with the predecessor's warning, which was half right.** It said
attention and time are the cultural analogues of an energy budget and are *not the
same thing*. Both halves stand. **The accounting carries**: attention is finite,
arrives at a rate, is contested. **Metabolism does not**: attention is not consumed
to stay alive, there is no first law over it, and nothing starves. Treating it as
food is still the category error. Treating it as scarce is not.

## What is fitness: the count of learners

An agent's cultural fitness for a variant is settled in the literature: **it is the
number of learners to whom it transmits that variant.** Not its wellbeing, not its
survival, not its offspring.

Directly countable, and this design had already named it as an instrument
(transmission chain depth) without noticing it was the fitness function. **We did
not have to invent this half and should not have tried**, which is exactly the trap
`CHARTER.md` warns about: the predecessor spent its budget re-deriving things the
literature had settled.

## What is welfare: the need vector, and it stays separate

The field's central finding is that **cultural variants can be optimised for their
own spread rather than for the welfare of their carriers.** A doctrine that makes
its persons miserable can outcompete one that does not, as long as it is
transmitted more.

⚠ **If welfare were the fitness function, that would be unrepresentable by
construction, and this would be a model in which culture is necessarily good for
the persons in it.**

Keeping the two apart is what makes a parasitic culture expressible. It is the same
shape as the model-doctrine split: two things usually assumed to agree, held apart
so that their disagreement can be measured. The gap between what spreads and what
is good for its carriers is the thing worth studying, and it exists only if they
are separate numbers.

## The material base, at the smallest honest size

Subsistence and protection need material conditions, and material conditions were
the energy economy that consumed twenty-four worlds. The minimum that is still
honest:

**Carrying capacity is one number, not a resource field.** An island supports so
many persons; being over it means subsistence goes unmet for somebody. No grid, no
food chemistry, no metabolism, no first law.

And `max_persons` **already exists in `border.erl`** as the reason an island turns
somebody away, which makes the border's *we are full* a material fact rather than
an arbitrary policy. **That is cultural materialism in miniature, at the cost of a
number already written.**

## Three economic forms, as a target rather than a setting

Polanyi identified three modes of economic integration: **reciprocity,
redistribution, and market exchange.**

Which mode an island arrives at is a finding. Nothing here imposes one, and *an
island evolved redistribution* is the kind of result this design exists to be able
to produce. It is also a concrete, named, published target rather than a vague hope
that something interesting will happen.
