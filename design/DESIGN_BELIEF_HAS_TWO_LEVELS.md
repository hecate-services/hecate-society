# Belief has two levels, and their disagreement is the subject

**Decision: a person holds a `model`, written only by observation, and a
`doctrine`, written only by other persons.**

Ruled 2026-08-04. It had been a proposal for some hours before that and is now
settled.

---

## The two stores

| | what it is | who may write to it |
|---|---|---|
| **model** | what this person's own experience taught them about how the world behaves | observation, and nothing else |
| **doctrine** | what this person was told, held with a confidence | other persons, and nothing else |

Behaviour comes from both. **The gap between them is the phenomenon**: dissonance,
conversion, radicalisation, and the conditions under which evidence defeats what
you were taught or fails to.

A belief held in spite of the evidence is where everything interesting lives, and
**a single merged belief store cannot represent one.** That is the whole argument
for paying for two stores instead of one.

## Doctrine is a set, not a slot

It maps propositions to confidences, and being told something **writes an entry
without clearing the others**.

A person can therefore hold two doctrines in tension as well as a doctrine against
their own model. That is what conversion looks like from the inside and what
syncretism looks like from outside, and it is how conquest actually works: Rome
did not erase local religion, it added the imperial cult on top.

An earlier draft of this design had doctrine as a slot that conquest overwrote.
That was wrong, and it would have thrown away the convert who still keeps the old
feast days. **It also gives a third comparison for free:** model against doctrine,
and doctrine against doctrine, which is a person who has been told two
incompatible things and is a measurable state.

## Why not a neural network

A network was considered for this role and rejected. **A network maps a situation
onto a response.** It cannot hold a proposition that can be stated, contested,
taught, or believed against what is in front of it.

⚠ **The rejection is about beliefs only.** Behaviour is a network, it is unbounded,
and it is described in
[DESIGN_THE_PERSON_IS_A_NETWORK.md](DESIGN_THE_PERSON_IS_A_NETWORK.md). The two
decisions look contradictory read separately and are not: **the network is the
policy, the belief stores are data it reads and writes.**

## A proposition must be checkable against the world

It has the shape *acting this way in that situation yields this outcome*, and the
world either behaves that way or does not.

**This is a design requirement before it is anything else.** If a proposition has
no truth in the world then the model cannot contradict the doctrine, and the only
thing worth measuring here is unmeasurable.

It also settles what a proposition may be ABOUT. A statement whose truth is not
decided by this world's own physics has no truth here at all, so nothing can
contradict it, so it can never generate the disagreement this design exists to
measure. That is a modelling constraint and it needs no other justification.

## The pipeline this implies, which the literature has already worked out

Self-determination theory has an **internalisation continuum**: external
regulation, then introjected, then identified, then integrated, describing how a
rule imposed from outside becomes part of who somebody is.

That is the doctrine-to-model pipeline, already described and measured for
decades. **Conversion is not something this project has to invent a rule for.**
SDT was not adopted as the need axes, for reasons in
[DESIGN_NEEDS_AND_SATISFIERS.md](DESIGN_NEEDS_AND_SATISFIERS.md), but this part of
it transfers directly.
